Mogisari Shrine (Courage to Jump) in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a shrine located in the Akkala Sky region. This page contains a guide for how to locate and enter the shrine, a walkthrough for the shrine itself, and puzzle solutions as well as treasure chest locations for the TotK Mogisari shrine. Mogisari Shrine is outside the **Lomei Sky Labyrinth**. Check out more TOTK shrine locations here. 

### Treasure and Rewards

  * Mighty Zonaite Shield



## Location/How to Reach

![](image-002.jpg)![](image-003.jpg)

Mogisari Shrine is located on the south side of the Lomei Sky Labyrinth in the far, far northeast corner of Hyrule. To get there from the surface, the closest tower is the Ulri Mountain Skyview Tower, but it’s a LONG WAY to the Shrine. You will either have create and eat a lot of stamina-increasing food to get there, or use Zonai devices. 

Your target either way is a small sky island to the southwest of the labyrinth with a tower and a pool of water on it. 

![](image-004.jpg)![](image-005.jpg)![](image-006.jpg)

If you choose the stamina route: You’ll need about 4 rings of stamina to get here with the paraglider from the Skyview Tower. Cook these items and float to the island with the pool, north of the tower. This is the North Akkala Sky Archipelago. 

![](image-007.jpg)![](image-008.jpg)![](image-009.jpg)

If you choose the Zonai device route: Start at the Gikaku Shrine (go to the Gikaku Shrine Guide to see how to get there from the Skyview Tower). 

Use the stone launcher to get up to the second level of the Sky Mine. Here is a floating Zonai platform with two rockets and a Construct on it. Kill the Construct and get on the platform. Affix the two rockets to its surface, aimed at the North Akkala Sky Archipelago. 

![](image-010.jpg)![](image-011.jpg)![](image-012.jpg)

Now, with the rockets firmly affixed, move the platform with Ultrahand allll the way to the north side of the island. 

Get on the platform and activate the rockets to take you about half of the way to the island. From here you are high enough that you should be able to float down. If you have no stamina expansions, try using another rocket, or fan, affixed to the platform. 

![](image-013.jpg)

Now, safely land on the small island in the North Akkala Sky Archipelago, you will need an air ship to get you to the Labyrinth entrance above. Luckily, there’s one here. Among the **Small Fairies** is a perfectly useable fan-plane, and look down the steps on the north side for tons of extra batteries. 

![](image-014.jpg)![](image-015.jpg)![](image-016.jpg)

A total of 8 batteries will get you there easily. The last leg of the journey will be assisted by low gravity as well! 

![](image-017.jpg)![](image-018.jpg)![](image-019.jpg)

  * Coordinates: 4654, 3499, 1010



## Walkthrough and Puzzle Solutions

In this low gravity Shrine you will be able to jump further – whether with your feet or on a vehicle. 

![](image-020.jpg)![](image-021.jpg)

Jump down and get on a vehicle below. Head down the track, using the jump to span the first gap. 

Pilot over the lava, past the Constructs, and over the spikes. 

You can drive right over the lasers. Just past the lasers, go LEFT at the fork. Here you will see a cracked stone wall on a small building.   


## Treasure Chest Location

After taking the left fork past the lasers, ram the stone wall with your car. It will break and reveal a chest with a Mighty Zonaite Shield inside. 

![](image-022.jpg)![](image-023.jpg)

Now get back in the car and stick left at the fork near the end. Stop here by the rockets. 

![](image-024.jpg)

Attack two rockets to the rear of the vehicle to clear the final jump. Activate the car and blast over the final ramp and to the exit platform.
