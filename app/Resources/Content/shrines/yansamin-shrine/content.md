Yansamin Shrine (**Proving Grounds: Low Gravity**) in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a located on Zonaite Forge Island in the East Necluda Sky region and one of 152 shrines in TOTK (see all shrine locations). See what treasures the Yansamin Shrine hides and, of course, how to fight your way through the odd Yansamin Shrine with this complete guide. 

![](image-001.png)

### Yansamin Treasure and Rewards

  * Large Zonaite



## Yansamin Shrine Location

Yansamin Shrine is located on Zonaite Forge Island in the East Necluda Sky, north of Rabella Wetlands Skyview Tower. If you have Zora Armor equipped, Link can swim up a waterfall located west of the Peak of Awakening in Lanyru. 

![](image-002.png)![](image-003.png)

Run across the sky island to the floating platform. Use Ultrahand to attach a Rocket to the platform and strike it to fly up. Use the Paraglider to travel straight to Zonaite Forge Island. 

![](image-004.png)![](image-005.png)

Touch the green hand to activate the fans on the island. Use the Paraglider to travel from fan to fan until you reach the center cylinder with a hole in the middle. 

![](image-006.png)![](image-007.png)![](image-008.png)

Jump into the hole while avoiding the red lasers to land in the water. This is a spring that will heal you if you swim around in it. Activate the shrine. 

![](image-009.png)

  * Coordinates: 2350, -1782, 1475



## Yansamin Shrine Walkthrough

This shrine is a proving grounds challenge, which means **outside equipment will be removed upon entry**. On the left of the entrance, pick up the provided Old Wooden Shield, Long Stick, and Wooden Stick. Head to the launching platform and wait on top of it until it shoots Link into the air. 

![](image-010.png)![](image-011.png)

After hitting the top, you’ll fall back down. Use your Paraglider to travel forward to a platform in the back right corner of the room with a Construct enemy. Immediately use******Fuse****to attach the Spiked Iron Ball to your equipped weapon**. Defeat the Construct with your new weapon. 

![](image-012.png)![](image-013.png)

Paraglide down to the floor next to the Fan device. Use Ultrahand to position it where you want and use the Paraglider to fly up in the air. Land on the middle platform with two Rockets and one Construct. 

![](image-014.png)![](image-015.png)

Defeat the Construct and use Fuse to attach one of the Rockets to your shield. Use the shield to propel Link into the air and land on the platform with three Constructs. Defeat the enemies. 

![](image-016.png)![](image-017.png)

Jump on the floating square platforms to reach the next Construct. If you fall, Fuse another Rocket to your shield and fly up to it. Defeat the enemy. 

![](image-018.png)![](image-019.png)

Use the Paraglider to reach the far back platform and defeat the two Construct enemies. 

![](image-020.png)

The final Construct is at the top platform. Land on the platform right below it and use Ascend to travel up. When you defeat it, the gate will open. 

![](image-021.png)![](image-022.png)![](image-023.png)

Paraglide down to the floor. Enter the room to open the treasure chest with a Large Zonaite inside. Exit the shrine.
