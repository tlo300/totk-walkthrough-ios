The Tears of the Kingdom side quest called Mattison's Independence is all about family. During this wholesome little story, you'll have to answer a difficult question, trick a railcar guy, and find ten sundelions. 

This Tears of the Kingdom **Mattison's Independence** walkthrough will show you how to do all that and more. 

## Mattison's Independence Walkthrough

To start Mattison's Independence, go to Lake Akkala in the Akkala Highlands and visit the small peninsula called **Tarrey Town**. Interact with Rhondson and Hudson as they're having a conversation in front of their home (coordinates 3937, 1590, 0129). 

Next, **speak to Hudson alone**. He'll explain that his daughter, Mattison, is leaving for Gerudo Town. He then asks you to spend some time with Mattison so she'll feel less lonely. Go up to the second floor of Hudson's house to find Mattison in her room. 

After a brief conversation, **follow Mattison** outside to meet the elderly couple. When Mattison asks what the Gerudo word for "granny" is, the correct answer is **vaba**. 

Follow Mattison again and **help her get on the railcar** without the operator, Hagie, noticing. You can grab a nearby wooden plank using your Ultrahand ability and place it in front of Hagie, then tell Mattison to get on the railcar. If the railcar isn't there, glide down and ride it upwards. 

Next, use your glider to reach Hudson's workplace (to the west), and speak to him. He has made an air balloon for Mattison, but he needs **ten sundelions** to dye it yellow. You can offer the sundelions right away if you already have them, or you can use our sundelion guide to find more. 

To complete Mattison's Independence, simply go through the remaining dialogue. You'll be awarded **two silver rupees** , which is equal to 200 green rupees.
