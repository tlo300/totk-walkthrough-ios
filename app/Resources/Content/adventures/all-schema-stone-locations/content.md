Schema Stones in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom are a Key Item that unlocks build designs, but you won't be able to use these, or see the specific design it unlocks, until you've unlocked a certain ability by completing the main quest A Mystery in the Depths. This page includes exactly how to use Schema Stones, how to find them, and all 12 Schema Stone locations. Schema Stones work similarly to Yiga Schematics. 

### Spoiler Warning

If you wish to not know about the ability you need to use Schema Stones, turn back now. 

Schema Stone

These stone slabs depict schematics of things such as Zonai vehicles. Autobuild saves the schematic and builds it on command.

## How to Use Schema Stones in TotK

You won't be able to use a Schema Stone or see what it grants until you have unlocked Autobuild, which you can get by completing A Mystery in the Depths, or, you can unlock Autobuild early by going to a specific location in the depths. 

Once you have Autobuild, when you pick up a Schema Stone, you'll see what it unlocks. 

  1. Unlock Autobuild
  2. Open the radial menu and equip Autobuild
  3. Scroll down to the "Schematics" menu to see the schematics you've unlocked so far
  4. Choose the schematic you want to build



And that's it! Remember, you'll need the Zonai Devices or enough Zonaite to create these contraptions. 

## Schema Stone Locations

There are 12 Schema Stones - see all of their locations on the map of Schema Stone Locations below. You'll mostly get Schema Stones from Constructs at various mines in the Depths, but two are found on the surface.

###  Schema Stone 1

Schema Stone 1 - Fanplane

![](image-004.png)

![](image-005.jpg)![](image-006.jpg)

This Schema Stone will be located in the Great Abandoned Central Mine which is surrounded by chasms in the Great Plateau above it. Talking to the Steward Construct here gives you the Fanplane build. This is your first stop in the Side Adventure, Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan, which can be started after completing the A Mystery in the Depths main quest. 

## Schema Stone 2

Schema Stone 2 - Launch Pad

![](image-007.png)

![](image-008.jpg)![](image-009.jpg)

This Schema Stone is located at the Abandoned Kakariko Mine, which is right below Kakariko Village in the east-central part of the Depths. It's just southwest of the East Hill Chasm. Talk to the Construct to get the Launch Pad build. 

## Schema Stone 3

Schema Stone 3 - Instant Scaffold

![](image-010.png)

![](image-011.jpg)![](image-012.jpg)

This Schema Stone is located at the Abandoned Kara Kara Mine, which is in the southwest area of the Depths and just northeast of the Abandoned Gerudo Mine. You will have to beat a Yiga soldier before you can talk to the construct nearby. This stone will give you the Instant Scaffold build. 

## Schema Stone 4

Schema Stone 4 - Hovercraft

![](image-013.png)

This Schema Stone is located at the **Abandoned Gerudo Mine** , which is near the southwest corner of the Depths and just southwest of the Abandoned Kara Kara Mine. Talk to the Steward Construct here to get the Hovercraft schematic build. 

## Schema Stone 5

Schema Stone 5 - Bridge

![](image-014.png)

![](image-015.jpg)![](image-016.jpg)

This Schema Stone will be located at the Abandoned Hateno Mine and will give you the Bridge build. You will have to beat a Yiga soldier before you can talk to the construct. 

## Schema Stone 6

Schema Stone 6 - Dirigible

![](image-017.png)

![](image-018.jpg)

Find the Dirigible Schema Stone at the Abandoned Lurelin Mine in the southeast corner of the depths. Talk to the Steward Construct to get it. ![](image-019.jpg)

## Schema Stone 7

Schema Stone 7 - Rocket Platform

This Schema Stone is at the Abandoned Hebra Mine in the depths, but getting there is a bit tricky. It can only be accessed by the Rito Village Chasm, which is inside a hidden cave. 

![](image-020.jpg)

From the Gatakis Shrine, head down the path until you reach a ledge. Then jump down again to the right (south) and paraglide, but turn and look at the cliffside pointed at below. 

![](image-021.jpg)

There is an opening there. Inside is the Chasm. 

Jumping down the chasm will bring you to the Abandoned Hebra Mine in the Depths. Speak to the Steward Construct at the Abandoned Hebra Mine to get the Rocket Platform Schematics. If you do the Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan side adventure that's unlocked after completing A Mystery in the Depths, you will eventually be led here! 

![](image-022.png)

## Schema Stone 8

Schema Stone 8 - Automated Ally

![](image-023.png)

The Automated Ally schema stone is at the Abandoned Eldin Mine in the northwest area of the Depths. You'll need Flame Guard (fire resistance) to explore this area. Get two pieces of the **Flameguard Armor Set** or make a Fireproof Elixir using Fireproof Lizards. 

A series of statues will lead you to the entrance of this mine. Talk to the Steward Construct to get the Schema Stone for the Automated Ally. 

## Schema Stone 9

Schema Stone 9 - Instant Cannon

![](image-024.png)

This Schema Stone can be found at the Abandoned Tarry Mine in the northeast part of the Depths. The closest chasm seems to be East Akkala Plains Chasm. From there, head south, traveling east if you encounter impassible walls. Eventually, you should see a large tunnel if you look west. 

The Kawikatisar Lightroot is the closest lightroot, but if you come across it from the East Akkala Plains Chasm, you've gone too far south. Luckily, activating that Lightroot will illuminate where the Abandoned Tarry Mine is on the map - just north of the Kawikatisar Lightroot. 

Talk to the Steward Construct there to get the Instant Cannon Schema Stone. 

## Schema Stone 10

Schema Stone 10 - Bolt Boat

![](image-025.png)

The Abandoned Lanayru Mine where this Schema Stone lies is pretty well hidden - if you do the Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan side adventure that's unlocked after completing A Mystery in the Depths, you will eventually be led here! You can travel from the Lanayru Wetlands Chasm and travel east, stopping by the Sekioam Lightroot on the way, before eventually cutting north to find the small opening - luckily, statues point toward it. 

However, the true closest lightroot is Kegopa Lightroot, which is fairly easily findable from the Kawikatisar Lightroot near the Abandoned Tarrey Mine. Talk to the Steward Construct on the platform to get the Bolt Boat Schema Stone. 

## Schema Stone 11

Schema Stone 11 - Hot Air Balloon

Complete the Main Quest called A Mystery in the Depths to get the Hot-Air Baloon Schema Stone. 

## Schema Stone 12

Schema Stone 12 - Beam Cycle

The entrance of the Shrine of Resurrection (a cave, not really a shrine), is up a cliff on the Great Plateau. Find it by heading to Riogok Shrine, climb up the cliff south of it, and then follow the ledge east until you see a Blupee. It will lead you to the entrance covered with leaves. 

The cave is a straight line, but in the back, there's a hole behind a small pond. Drop down it--but be prepared, you'll have to take out three Yiga Clan members before you can claim your prize of the Beam Cycle Schema Stone.
