A Letter to Koyin is a Side Adventure in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom in which you help Koyin retrieve a letter in a bottle, and learn some other helpful information in the process. Side Adventures are mini quests that tell a story. 

## A Letter to Koyin

To start “A Letter to Koyin,” speak to Koyin at Hateno Pasture, where she’s usually standing at the edge of the pond. She says she needs to retrieve the message in a bottle from the pond, and luckily you came along just in time to help. 

![](image-001.png)

The bottle is right in the center of the pond, so it’s up to you to figure out how to get it out, but if you need help, we recommend cutting down a few trees in the forest, fusing them together end to end. Then send your makeshift staff into the water with Ultrahand and fuse it to the bottle, pulling it back out of the water after. 

![](image-002.png)

If you have a fan to spare, you can also fuse the logs into a raft to retrieve the bottle. If you’re lacking an axe to make logs, there are sharp stones around in the forest, so you can fuse one together. 

Once you retrieve the bottle, drop it next to Koyin and she’ll read it. She instructs you to meet her at her home for your reward, so follow her. From the recipe in the bottle, Koyin has made Hateno Cheese, and is now willing to sell it for 20 Rupees or trade it to you for some Fresh Milk. 

![](image-003.png)

If you’ve already helped with “Dantz’s Prize Cows” you can effectively trade 3 acorns for Hateno Cheese. As luck would have it, it’s a necessary component for Mayor Reede’s so head over to him if you want to help him in “A New Signature Food.”
