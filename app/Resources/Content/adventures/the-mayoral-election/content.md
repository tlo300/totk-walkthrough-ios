The Mayoral Election is a Side Adventure in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom in which you resolve the feud between Cece and Reede. Side Adventures are mini quests that tell a story. 

## The Mayoral Election

If you’ve completed all of the Side Adventures related to Cece and Reede, it’s time to resolve the election! This quest will begin as soon as you complete the last of the quests related to the election. For convenience, we’ve listed them below. 

  * Team Cece or Team Reede?
  * A New Signature Food
    * A Letter to Koyin (required to complete A New Signature Food)
  * Cece’s Secret
  * Reede’s Secret



Now speak to Sophie, she should be right outside Ventest Clothing Boutique, but she’ll be marked on the minimap if you select The Mayoral Election as your main quest. After the ensuing dramatic scenes, follow Cece to her shop to accept your reward: the Cece Hat that she was designing when you began this whole questline. Additionally, Cece is now selling clothes and can replace unique clothes for a fee, if you've misplaced or sold them!
