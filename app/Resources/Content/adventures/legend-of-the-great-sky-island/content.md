Legend of the Great Sky Island is one of the many Side Adventures in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. It's the only Side Adventure on The Great Sky Island and one of the few Side Quests/Side Adventures that begin and end on the sky islands above Hyrule in TotK. 

A Steward Construct tells a Link a tale about how the Great Island and the Temple of Time floated into the skies. The Construct tests Link with an obstacle course. 

  * **Side Adventure Location:** The Great Sky Island - Top of the Temple of Time.
  * **Prerequisites:** n/a
  * **Rewards:** Zonai Fabric



## How to Complete the Legend of The Great Sky Island

Before returning to The Great Sky Island above Central Hyrule, it's best to have high stamina and complete the Restoring the Zora Armor Quest. You will need the **Zora Armor** to complete this quest. You will also need some Fire Fruit, Red Chuchu Jelly, or anything that can be attached to an arrow and start a fire. 

When you return to The Great Sky Island, you will spot green smoke on top of the Temple of Time. Glide to the top to find a Steward Construct waiting for you. 

After speaking with the Construct, he will ask Link to complete an obstacle course. If you accept the Construct's challenge, the Construct will force the time to change to 7 AM and raise several land masses into the sky. All the land masses will have waterfalls dripping from them. 

The goal of the Construct's mission is to glide across The Great Sky Island and **ignite the three bonfires** scattered across the island. The challenge of this mission is that **you can't touch the ground** and you only have 12 in-game hours to complete, that is why the Construct turns the day to 7 AM. 

All three bonfires are marked on your map. The Zora armor will allow you to ride up the waterfalls from the small floating land masses. Use the waterfalls to propel yourself in the air and regain your stamina. 

When you glide nearby a bonfire, take out your bow and arrow and fuse an item that will cause a fire on impact, like a Fire Fruit. Shoot the bonfire to ignite, and make sure to take out your paraglider again to glide again. 

Once you've ignited all three bonfires, return to the top of the Temple of Time to talk to the Construct again. You still can't touch the ground as you return to the Temple. 

The Construct will thank you and reward you with a **Zonai Fabric**. You can use the fabric to customize your paraglider.
