**Hestu's Concerns** is one of the many Side Adventures you can embark on in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know about completing the Hestu's Concerns Side Adventure, including where to find him and how exactly to ease his concerns. 

To trigger the Hestu's Concerns Side Quest, you must first find **Hestu**. Hestu can be found in a few different locations, so where exactly you come across him will depend on the trajectory of your adventure. 

Hestu can be found trembling before some enemies near Lindor's Brow in Hyrule Ridge, or standing by a tree in Lookout Landing. Wherever you come across Hestu first, speak to him to trigger the Hestu's Concern Side Adventure. 

Hestu's main concern is that he has somehow lost the **Korok seeds** that gave his maracas their signature sound. Hestu explains that while he was in a panic, the Koroks took the seeds and hid them around Hyrule. 

Find and give Hestu back at least one Korok seed to complete the Side Adventure, and Hestu will perform a song and dance for you that **increases your inventory size** by one slot. 

Hestu's Concerns

You can find Hestu in Lookout Landing after you've completed his quest, and he will continue to upgrade your inventory in exchange for Korok seeds.
