A New Signature Food is a Side Adventure in **The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom****** in which you resolve a feud that’s dividing Hateno Village. This guide walks you through how to complete A New Signature Food in Zelda TotK. 

## TotK A New Signature Food Walkthrough

![](image-001.png)

To start the “A New Signature Food” Side Adventure in Zelda TotK, you first need to start the Side Adventure “**Team Cece or Team Reede?**” 

Once the quest has started, you can find Reede to give him some help as well. He can often be found in his home next door to the inn, and he wants help in recreating a dish from his past that will revitalize his supporters and earn some new ones. 

![](image-002.png)

Unfortunately, he can’t remember the recipe or the dish exactly. Your only clues are a creamy, melty texture, but he also recommends visiting the local farm, so head up to Hateno Pasture. The farm is halfway up the path to the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab to the east. 

Although you may be tempted to talk to Dantz about Reede’s issue, you actually need to speak to his daughter, Koyin, at the edge of the pond and help her to complete the Side Adventure “A Letter for Koyin.” 

![](image-003.png)

Once you’ve successfully helped Koyin, you can bring back the Hateno Cheese to Reede for a handsome **100 Rupee reward** and the end of the quest! This is one of the quests required to unlock the final quest of the Hateno Election questline, "**The Mayoral Election**."
