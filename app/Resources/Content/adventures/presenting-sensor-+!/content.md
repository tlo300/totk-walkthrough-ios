Presenting: Sensor +! is a side adventure focused on upgrading your Purah Pad and making it easier to find the exact things and monsters you need the most. To do the quest, you have to first unlock the Camera ability and finish the Hateno Village Research Lab quest at the lab topped with the big telescope at the top of Hateno Village. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Hateno Village Research Lab
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Camera Work in the Depths, Hateno Village Research Lab
  * **Rewards:** Sensor + ability added (accessible from the Maps screen)



After fishing the Research Lab quest, speak to Robbie again and select the Sensor +? dialog option to learn more about it. He will tell you that you can unlock this function once you have gathered information on five different types of monsters. 

## How to use Sensor +

The Sensor + lets you target anything you have logged in your Compendium via the Purah Pad proximity sensor. Need to find certain ingredients or monster parts to brew potions -- either target the monster itself or the component and the sensor will beep whenever you're near. 

Note that the sensor only chimes when you're moving. Once you hear the alert, pinpoint the right direction by watching the proximity meter (the more bars, the closer you are). If you're near the location, note also that the sensor will tell you that the target is either above or below you. 

## Tip: How to Find Caves With the Sensor +

Use your knowledge of wildlife to find related locations. For example, you know that Bubbulfrogs are found in caves. Set your Sensor + target to a Bubbulfrog to locate hidden caves you may not yet have spotted.
