**Fell into a Well!** is a short Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom side quest found in Central Hyrule near the Rebonae Bridge Well. The quest starting point is in the Rebonae Bridge Well itself and is easily spotted from the road leading out of Central Hyrule due to the smoke emitting from the well itself. 

You'll need to climb or glide down the well to avoid taking damage as there is no water below the well's entrance. Once down, you'll see a man lamenting the unfortunate state of the well's ladder. It's broken! 

Speak to the man called Dillie. He's instantly elated, as he should be! You're here to save the day. 

To complete the Fell into a Well Quest, all you need to do is use Ultrahand to pick up the broken ladder piece and reconnect it with the hanging piece. Note that the ladder on the ground is made of several pieces that swing, so be sure to grab it from one of its ends to attach it properly. 

Once the ladder is properly attached, speak to Dillie. They all exit the well, and as thanks for a safe escape, Dillie offers a Purple Rupee (50 rupees!).
