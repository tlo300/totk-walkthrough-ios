**Horse-Drawn Dreams** is one of many Side Quests in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom that can be found in the **Hyrule Ridge region**. This quest kicks off at the New Serenne Stable near the North Hyrule Plane road and serves as a tutorial for catching horses and connecting them to wagons. 

  * **Quest Giver** : Zumi
  * **Prerequisite** : None
  * **Reward** : Tap to Reveal



Zumi stands to the southeast of the stable near the road. She stands looking at a wagon, one she'll tell you she's wanted after a tireless search. Unfortunately for her, it's not functional. 

After talking with Zumi you learn you need two things: 

  * Two wheels for the wagon
  * A horse for Zumi



### Finding Wagon Wheels

Zumi mentions that every stable has various building materials. This is true for New Serenne Stable! There are actually three wagon wheels **just beyond the wagon, laying next to the side of the stable by a bucket**. Use Ultrahand to reveal them! 

Use Ultrahand to attach wheels to the two open spots on the wagon. 

Zumi talks to Link unprompted once you successfully attach the two wagon wheels. There's only one task left. 

### Catching a Wild Horse

Wild horses roam just north of New Serenne Stable. In fact, you may be able to look just across the road and see some wandering around North Hyrule Plain! 

To catch a wild horse you'll need to... 

  * Slowly approach it, caring not to scare it. If you startle a wild horse while behind it, it may kick you!
  * When you're close enough to a horse, crouch to move quietly toward it.
  * Press A when you see the "Mount" prompt.
  * Press LB to soothe the wild horse, taming it. It won't be fully tamed, however. If it starts shaking its head or veers away from the direction you want to go, press LB again and correct it. Basically, you want to reassure the horse and praise it when it does something you like.



Once you have a horse controlled, ride it to Zumi. When you're close enough press ZL + A to target and speak with her while mounted on the horse. She'll confirm whether or not you intend to give her the horse before taking it. At this point she'll explain a little more about horse harnesses too. 

Zumi then rewards Link with a Silver Rupee, a single rupee worth 100 rupees! With that, you've completed the quest. You can talk to Zumi again to hear about the horse Zunk and more generally about handling horses.
