The Unknown Sky Giant sidequest is the fourth of six monster-hunting quests available from the head of the monster-control crew, Gralens. Each quest is a lucrative, netting Link 100 Rupees as a reward for slaying the bounty. Link additionally receives a Silver Rupee for completing the first three, and a Diamond for the second set of missions. 

To find Gralens, you have to first complete the Bring Peace to Hyrule Field quest. The monster-controller will then be a resident of the Emergency Shelter at the center of Lookout Landing. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Lookout Landing, Central Hyrule
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Bring Peace to Hyrule Field and the three WANTED quests
  * **Rewards:** Silver Rupee (100), and a Gold Rupee (300) for the completion of all three WANTED quests.



Talk to Gralens, who is standing by the table near the map in the underground Emergency Center in the middle of Lookout Landing. Ask Gralens about "Unusual monsters" -- an option that is available only after you complete the three "big monsters" WANTED quests. 

You can then accept the three quests: **Unknown Sky Giant** , Unknown Three-Headed Monster, and Unknown Huge Silhouette. You should accept all three while you're here. 

Accept Unknown Sky Giant and Gralen will give you the location of a Flux Construct I on the West Hebra Sky Archipelago. Travel to the closest location to the battle arena on your sky map and head to the blinking dot marker. 

## How to Easily Defeat the Flux Construct I

Flux Construct I is the easiest of the three different Flux Construct types, a Zonaite-powered robot made up of single blocks. It first appears as a sentry tower. You can safely attack it from afar by shooting the eye block on top. It will quickly reform into a walking robot shape. Keep track of the special power block. Use Ultrahand to lock on to it and yank it out of the Construct. This will scatter all the blocks. Run over to the power block and attack it. 

The Construct will likely shift to its big block form now, rolling steadily towards you in an attempt to squash Link. The power block remains vulnerable to attacks and you can safely attack it from afar with a bomb arrow or similar. You can also patiently wait for the power block to be accessible to your Ultrahand again -- just keep running away with the camera trained on the Flux Construct I. You can also remove single regular blocks to create holes and make it easier to see the power block. Your choice. 

Its third attack phase consists of a hovering single layer of blocks. If it's close to the ground you can Ultrahand-grab the power block once again to be able to isolate it and attack it. The Flux Constract will also launch a row of blocks at you from above. You can evade these blocks, climb on top of one, and the rewind time with Recall to get up on top of your enemy. This makes it easy to attack the power block. 

Once you have defeated the Flux Construct I, collect your spoils, travel back to Lookout Landing and turn in the quest to receive 100 Rupees. 

If this is the third of the Unusual Monsters quests you've completed, you'll also receive a valuable Diamond.
