## How to Find Voice Memories in TotK

Voice Memories are a collectible in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom which can only be found using the Zelda Notes app, included in the Nintendo Switch app for mobile devices. 

A membership to Nintendo Switch Online is not required to use Zelda Notes. However, you must own the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition of The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom in order to use Zelda Notes.

To find a Voice Memory in TotK, open your Nintendo Switch app and select The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom Nintendo Switch 2 Edition from the Game Specific Services section. From here, select the Voice Memories tile in the top left corner. 

Select the Voice Memory you would like to locate and use Navigation to find your way there. Once you walk into the immediate area of the Voice Memory, the narration from that Memory’s character will play from your mobile device automatically. Make sure you don’t have your phone’s volume muted!
