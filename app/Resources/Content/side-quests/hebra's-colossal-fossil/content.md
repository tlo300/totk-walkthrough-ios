**Hebra’s Colossal Fossil** is one of the many Side Quests to complete in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know to complete Hebra’s Colossal Fossil, including where to find it and what you will get as a reward. 

  * **Side Quest Location** : Hebra North Summit, Hebra Mountains
  * **Prerequisites** : Complete **Eldin’s Colossal Fossil**
  * **Rewards** : 50 Rupees



After completing **Eldin’s Colossal Fossil** , **Loone** will travel to the **Hebra Great Skeleton** (-3823, 3602, 0257) near the**Hebra North Summit**. Not only is there a Shrine nearby for traveling, but the entrance to the cave is somewhat ceremonial with an ornate archway. 

Once inside, speak to Loone to get the low down on what this skeleton is supposed to be. It’s missing a **fin bone and a vertebra of its spine/tail** , so we’re gonna have to fix it. **Both of these bones are trapped in rocks, so you’ll probably want to bring along stone weapons or Bomb Flowers to clear them out**. If you didn’t, don’t worry, there are several Bomb Flowers and stones around to shoot or Fuse with things you have on you. 

The fin bone is trapped in rock not far away on the left side of Loone. Smash the rocks to get it out, then Ultrahand it and place it almost directly over to the right where it was missing. 

The vertebra is around the mid/back of the skeleton on the ground around the right side. Blow apart the rocks around it to find the bone in a pit in the ground. You might have to spin it a weird way to get it lined up with the other bones, but you should see a spot along the tail not too far away from the vertebra itself. 

The bones should slip into place when correct. Once they’re secure, speak to Loone again to have her swooning over the relics and she’ll reward you. She’ll then head off to the Gerudo Desert, where you can complete the final of her missions, Gerudo’s Colossal Fossil.
