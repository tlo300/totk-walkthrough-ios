WANTED: Hinox is the third of six monster-hunting quests available from the head of the monster-control crew, Gralens. Each quest is a lucrative, netting Link 100 Rupees as a reward for slaying the bounty. 

To find Gralens, you have to first complete the Bring Peace to Hyrule Field quest. The monster-controller will then be a resident of the Emergency Shelter at the center of Lookout Landing. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Lookout Landing, Central Hyrule
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Bring Peace to Hyrule Field
  * **Rewards:** Silver Rupee (100), and a Gold Rupee (300) for the completion of all three WANTED quests.



Talk to Gralens, who is standing by the table near the map in the underground Emergency Center in the middle of Lookout Landing. You can ask Gralens for the locations of any of his squads on expeditions, but to do this particular quest you need to select the "How about big monsters" dialog option. 

You can then accept the three quests: WANTED: Stone Talus, WANTED: Molduga, and **WANTED: Hinox**. 

Accept WANTED: Hinox and Gralens will provide you with the target's map location around Trilby Plain near Woodland Stable northeast of Hyrule Field. Fast-travel to Eldin Canyon Skyview Tower and float over to the location marked on your map. The Hinox is fast asleep, so you can get a first hit in pretty easily. 

## How to Easily Defeat Hinox

Hinoxes are giant cyclopses that can deal high damage by tossing objects, swiping, or even sitting on their enemies. Their weak point is obvious: the single, big eye in the middle of their oversized heads. Fire an arrow at the eye and the Hinox will fall down and can be attacked easily without fear of taking damage. Keese Eyeballs can help add homing capabilities to arrows to make it easier to hit the eye. Repeat this attack procedure to take down the Hinox. 

Once you have defeated the Trilby Plain Hinox, collect your spoils, travel back to Lookout Landing and turn in the quest to receive 100 Rupees. 

If this is the third of the WANTED quests you've completed, you'll also receive a Gold Rupee, worth 300.
