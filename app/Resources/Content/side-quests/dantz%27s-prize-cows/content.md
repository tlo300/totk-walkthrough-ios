“Dantz’s Prize Cows” is one of the Side Quests you’ll discover in Hateno Village. Side Quests are quests in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom that are relatively short or simple chores that can earn you small rewards or services. 

## Dantz’s Prize Cows

To start the Side Quest, “Dantz’s Prize Cows”, speak to Dantz in Hateno Pasture. Despite the farm being famous for its high-quality milk, Dantz is currently out of the food that allows his cows to produce the milk: acorns, 3 acorns to be exact. 

Dantz says Retsam Forest is full of squirrels and acorns, so if you don’t have enough you can head east from the farm to search for them. If you’ve collected enough, hand them in to Dantz to receive a bottle of Fresh Milk and complete the quest! From now on you can trade in 3 acorns for Fresh Milk!
