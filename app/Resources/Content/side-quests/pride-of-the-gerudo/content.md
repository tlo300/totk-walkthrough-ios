**Pride of the Gerudo** is one of the many Side Quests to complete in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know to complete Pride of the Gerudo, including where to find it and what you will get as a reward. 

![](image-001.png)

  * **Side Quest Location** : Gerudo Town, Gerudo Desert
  * **Prerequisites** : Finish the Gerudo Desert portion of Regional Phenomena and finish The Missing Owner
  * **Rewards** : Scimitar of the Seven, **Daybreaker Shield**



You can only activate this quest to get these legendary-ish weapons after rescuing **Isha** from her plight. Once she gets back, she flirts with you a teeny bit and lets you know that she can craft the weapons if you can collect the materials. **They come as a set, so you can’t just do one and not the other**. 

![](image-002.png)

The materials are 4 Diamonds, 10 flint, a Gerudo Scimitar, and a Gerudo Shield. Click on any of those materials there to go to our pages on those respective items. Flint is probably the easiest to farm, while Diamonds are obviously going to be a bit more rare and requires some strategy. 

![](image-003.png)

The Scimitar and Shield are spread over the desert, but there is a guaranteed place - if you complete The Mysterious Eighth, you get access to the **Statue of the Eighth Heroine Room** , which on every Blood Moon, will regenerate all the different Gerudo weapons and arms. 

![](image-004.png)

When you collect the materials, you can return to Isha and she’ll craft the items for you. **You can come back to her and have the items remade at any time as long as you have the materials.**
