**A Picture for the Closed Stable I** is one of two photo-taking side quests you can start at the closed-down Gerudo Canyon Stable. All you have to do is find the right location and snap a picture of the desired scenery and you'll get a reward. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Gerudo Canyon Stable, Gerudo Desert Gateway
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Camera Work in the Depths and Piaffe, Packed Away
  * **Rewards:** 1 Pony Point, 1 Chilly Mushroom Risotto



Find Gerudo Canyon Stable at the entrance to the Gerudo Desert. Once you've cleared the room of boxes via the Piaffe, Packed Away quest and learned about the stable's fate and learn that it's no longer in operation, you can look at the two empty frames on the wall. Piaffe will tell you that he wants a very specific image to fill the frame: 

Picture hint: a photo of Spectacle Rock when it appears to be smiling. The famed spot can be found on the map. The Rito who've flown over it say this grin is best seen from above. 

## Where is Spectacle Rock?

This picture is trickier than it sounds. You can't just head to nearby Spectacle Rock. You have to head to the sky. Switch to the Sky Map and access the East Gerudo Sky Archipelago, which is above and to the east of the Spectacle Rock location (the double-rock with the ringed body of water southeast of the Desert Gateway/stable). If you've completed the archipelago's challenge you should have unlocked Rakashog Shrine. 

## How Do You Get a Picture of Smiling Spectacle Rock?

Fast-travel to Rakashog Shrine and make your way to the left "arm" of the cross-shaped area, facing southwest. From the edge of the floating island, look down and you'll see that the nearby round platform and Spectacle Rock's features below form a smiling face. 

Take a picture -- you should see the "Smiling Spectacle Rock" marker highlight that it's the shot Piaffe wants. 

Now return to Gerudo Canyon Stable and walk to the frame on the wall and active it. Piaffe will tell you about the quest and again and you get to show him the photo. As a reward, you'll get a Pony Point and a Chilly Mushroom Risotto. 

If you haven't done it yet, you can move on to A Picture for Closed Stable II.
