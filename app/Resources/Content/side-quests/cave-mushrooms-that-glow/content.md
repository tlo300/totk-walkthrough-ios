**Cave Mushrooms That Glow** is a side quest in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom found in the Tabantha Frontier, specifically just east of Rito Village. It's a quick fetch quest that can be completed with a few button presses if you already have the required items! 

  * **Quest Giver** : Nat
  * **Prerequisite** : None
  * **Reward** : Tap to Reveal



Just off the road leading from Rito through the Tabantha Hills is a campsite with two women not far from it, standing in front of the mouth of a cave. Approach and you'll overhear Nat and Meghyn say they're looking for **10****Brightcap****mushrooms.**

If you already have at least 10 collected, Nat will turn to you and ask for them. If you don't have them, enter the cave to collect the glowing mushrooms. Just be careful! Inside is a Horriblin and a Like Like. They are guarding some treasure beyond the mushrooms, though! 

Nat rewards your generosity with a Spicy Tomato Mushroom Stew. They also hint at a secret... which you can learn more about with The Captured Tent guide.
