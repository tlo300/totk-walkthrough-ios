**The Horse Guard’s Request** is one of the many Side Quests to complete in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know to complete The Horse Guardian’s Request, including where to find it and what you will get as a reward. 

To trigger the Side Quest, head to **Outskirt Stable** and speak to a little old man named **Toffa**. On sunny days, Toffa can be found tending to the horses outside the stable. When it’s raining, he may seek shelter inside. 

Toffa is a lover of all horses - even the dead kind. After you speak to him, he tells you about some rumors he has heard about a group of **Stalhorses** that roam around the Saladin Park Ruins at night. And, of course, he would love to see one. 

Head North toward the **Sanadin Park Ruins** which are just over Safula Hill on the way to Hyrule Ridge. The horses appear at night in the clearing directly behind the large **Horse Sculpture**. 

Stalhorses only appear between the hours of **9.00 pm and 5.00 am** , so you may need to light a fire and wait until nighttime, depending on what time it is when you get there. 

Once you have located the Stalhorses, crouch down and sneak up behind one to mount it. There is no need to tame Stalhorses. They are already quite friendly and won’t try to buck you off. 

Once you have mounted your Stalhorse, ride it back to Outskirt Stable to show it to Toffa. Stalhorses always die at **5.00 am** every morning as the sun comes up, so try to head straight there. Time is of the essence. 

Once you reach Toffa at the Stable, press ZL to target and speak to him to show him the horse and complete the Side Quest. Toffa will give you a **Purple Rupee** and **one Pony Point** as a reward. 

Toffa is not satisfied with just seeing a Stalhose, though. Next up, he wants you to mount a **creature that isn’t a horse** and bring it to him. Toffa isn’t specific about the exact creature he wants to see you ride, just so long as it isn’t a horse. So you are free to decide if you would prefer to mount a deer or a bear, but a deer is certainly the easier option. 

You can find two deer in the bushes on the hill behind the Stable. Sneak up quietly behind one and mount it in the same way you would a horse. 

Deer are much more skittish and far harder to tame, so make sure you walk as quietly as you possibly can. Even then, they might still get spooked. Consider making a Sneaky Elixir or waiting until it's raining to make them easier to sneak up on. 

Take the deer down to Toffa to complete the Side Quest. He will give you a **Silver Rupee** and another **Pony Point** as a reward.
