The Unknown Huge Silhouette sidequest is the last of six monster-hunting quests available from the head of the monster-control crew, Gralens. Each quest rewards you with 100 Rupees -- with a bonus 300 Rupees for completing the first set of three, and a Diamond for the second one. 

To find Gralens, you have to first complete the Bring Peace to Hyrule Field quest. The monster-controller will then be a resident of the Emergency Shelter at the center of Lookout Landing. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Lookout Landing, Central Hyrule
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Bring Peace to Hyrule Field and the three WANTED quests
  * **Rewards:** Silver Rupee (100), and a Gold Rupee (300) for the completion of all three WANTED quests.



Talk to Gralens, who is standing by the table near the map in the underground Emergency Center in the middle of Lookout Landing. Ask Gralens about "Unusual monsters" -- an option that is available only after you complete the three "big monsters" WANTED quests. 

You can then accept the three quests: Unknown Sky Giant, Unknown Three-Headed Monster, and **Unknown Huge Silhouette**. You should accept all three while you're here. 

Accept Unknown Huge Silhouette to get a map marker in the Depths, northeast of the Great Abandoned Central Mine. Drop down the chasm to the south of Hyrule Field or travel to the nearest Lightroot you've got unlocked and head to the marked location. 

## How to Easily Defeat the Frox

Frox battles are very reminsicent of Hinox battles -- but with a little bit of Talus and classic Zelda Dodongo antics mixed in. A Frox is a Brightbloom-obsessed giant toad creature and attacks by using its massive body weight to crush Link. It also tries to swallow its enemies by breathing in and sucking in all the air and objects in its proximity. That means: keep your distance to avoid both of these attack patterns and only close in when it's safe. 

There are effective attack patterns. First, use arrows to shoot its eye -- just like with a Hinox. Fuse a Keese Eyeball to your arrow to get a guaranteed hit, but be careful not to fire when it's about to launch its vacuum cleaner attack. The latter has a very effective counter measure, but you have to move fast. Aim an arrow and fuse a bomb flower to it and fire into the creature's mouth -- or toss a Bomb or Time Bomb the old-fashioned way. If you miss your window, run away and to the side to avoid getting sucked in and swallowed. 

Both attacks yield the same result: Frox will get knocked out temporarily. This is the time to sprint in close, run or float on top of the Frox and use a heavy hammer-type weapon to attack the crystals on top of its back. The best strategy is to position yourself between two or more crystals and hold your attack to execute a continuous hammer spin and cause massive damage until the crystals shatter. 

Once you have defeated the Frox, collect your spoils, travel back to Lookout Landing and turn in the quest to receive 100 Rupees. 

If this is the last of the Unusual Monsters quests you've completed, you'll also receive a Diamond.
