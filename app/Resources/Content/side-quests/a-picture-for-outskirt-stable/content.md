A **Picture for Outskirt Stable** is a picture-taking side quest found at Outskirt Stable in Hyrule Field. You'll need to have the Camera ability unlocked to take on this mission. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Outskirt Stable, Hyrule Field
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Camera Work in the Depths
  * **Rewards:** 1 Pony Point, 1 Egg Tart



## A Picture for Outskirt Stable Walkthrough

Head to Outskirt Stable, which you can easily reach by fast-traveling to Tsutsu-um Shrine in Hyrule Field. Here's the exact map location for the shrine, in case you haven't discovered it yet: 

Head inside and go to the empty frame marked with the horse head on the wall (to the right of the map with the cherry trees). The stable's owner, Embry, will talk to you once you interact with it and tell you that'd he'd love to have the frame filled with a painting showing one of the mythical Cherry-Blossom Trees (that serve as a way to see the secret caves in each respective area). 

**Note:** Take a picture of the Cherry-Blossom Tree Map on the Outskirt Stable wall and keep it. You can also store it in the Switch Gallery to access it whenever you need a reminder where the nearest tree is. This isn't needed for any particular quest, but just a helpful guide you can refer back to or use to annotate your in-game map. 

Accept the quest from Embry and you'll get the hint that the tree is on Satori Mountain to the northwest. The tree is high up and easily spotted from the air or when climbing up the mountain. Look for it at this location north of the summit. 

When you're close to the Cherry-Blossom Tree with its pink petals, take a picture using the Camera ability. A text display tag with an exclamation mark in the top left corner of your viewfinder will tell you whether you're in the right spot. Snap the shot and return to Outskirt Stable. 

Interact with the picture frame and you can turn in the quest by showing off your photo. Embry will reward you with a Pony Point and an Egg Tart.
