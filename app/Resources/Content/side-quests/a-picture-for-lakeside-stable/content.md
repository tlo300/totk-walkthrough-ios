**A Picture for Lakeside Stable** is one of many Side Quests in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom that requires Link to fetch an image for the stable in question. This side quest begins at Lakeside Stable in Faron. 

  * **Quest Giver** : Anly
  * **Prerequisite** : None
  * **Reward** : Tap to Reveal



When you're ready to begin approach the empty frame with the stable emblem in the middle of it and select "Examine" by pressing A. You'll draw Anly's attention. He explains stable owners are putting up paintings for patrons to enjoy, and it's time for Lakeside Stable to get one too. Here's what he requests: 

_"When it's clear out—which, granted, isn't often around these parts—you can get a beautiful view of Floria Falls. The best place to see it is Floria Bridge outside the stable. Wouldn't the view from there make a nice painting?"'_

It's time to get to work. 

### A Photo of Floria Falls on a Clear Day

Since Anly doesn't ask for a particular time of day for the photo, all you really need to do is watch the weather forecast next to the minimap and **wait for the weather to clear up.** If clear weather is imminent, you can wander nearby and wait for the weather. If nothing is showing up, pass the time by taking a rest at the stable, or better yet, rest by the covered campfire next to the bridge and Hudson supplies. 

**We found the weather is often unpredictable, but the mornings yielded clear weather.**

You'll know the weather is good when you aim your camera at the falls and see the side quest exclamation point and side quest note in the viewfinder. 

With your photo taken, return to Lakeside Stable and approach them empty frame again. Show Anly your photo and he'll recreate it as a painting for the stable. 

Once done, he'll reward you with one Pony Point and Tough Tomato Seafood Soup, the stable's specialty.
