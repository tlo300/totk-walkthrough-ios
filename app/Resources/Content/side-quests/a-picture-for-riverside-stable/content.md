**A Picture for Riverside Stable** is one of many Side Quests in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom that requires Link to fetch an image for the stable in question. This side quest begins at Riverside Stable in Hyrule Field. 

  * **Quest Giver** : Ember
  * **Prerequisite** : None
  * **Reward** : Tap to Reveal



To start the side quest, approach the empty photo frame on the right side of the shrine and examine it by pressing A. Ember notices Link and explains that The Stable Association members have hung frames in preparation for putting up paintings. Ember asks for the following: 

_"My mind is made up. I know just what to hang on that wall...a picture of a mystical, blessed Great Goddess Statue!"_

Ember then explains that the statue is in a temple southwest of Hyrule Field. Let's get to it. 

### Great Goddess Statue for Riverside Stable

If you look to the southwest of Hyrule Field you'll find The Great Plateau, a location no horse can reach. And, more importantly, the Great Plateau is home to the Temple of Time Ruins and a Great Goddess Statue! 

If you've found other Great Goddess Statues in other areas already, you can instead grab a photo of one of those quickly and return to the stable. The photo does **not** need to be of the Great Goddess Statue in the Temple of Time Ruins. 

You can get to the Great Plateau by teleporting to a location in Hyrule Sky and diving or by scaling the side of the cliff. Even better, you can destroy some rocks at the front gate of the Great Plateau (near the Forest of Time) and clear the path that leads up and into the area. You'll meet a thing that was hidden behind the gate too. On your way to the temple, you'll likely encounter some monsters, but the Great Plateau is big enough to where you can dodge them too if needed. 

Once you've arrived at the Temple of Time Ruins, snap a photo of the statue and you can return to the Riverside Stable. 

Interact with the empty photo frame again and Ember will ask you about the photo. Give him yours and he'll reproduce the photo as a painting for the table. 

Ember gives you One Pony Point and Energizing Crab Stir-Fry, the stable's signature dish, as a reward.
