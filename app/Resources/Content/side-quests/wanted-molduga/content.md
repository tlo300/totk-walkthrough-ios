WANTED: Molduga is one of six monster-hunting quests available from the head of the monster-control crew, Gralens. Each quest is a lucrative, netting Link 100 Rupees as a reward for slaying the bounty. 

To find Gralens, you have to first complete the Bring Peace to Hyrule Field quest. The monster-controller will then be a resident of the Emergency Shelter at the center of Lookout Landing. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Lookout Landing, Central Hyrule
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Bring Peace to Hyrule Field
  * **Rewards:** Silver Rupee (100), and a Gold Rupee (300) for the completion of all three WANTED quests.



Talk to Gralens, who is standing by the table near the map in the underground Emergency Center in the middle of Lookout Landing. You can ask Gralens for the locations of any of his squads on expeditions, but to do this particular quest you need to select the "How about big monsters" dialog option. 

You can then accept the three quests: WANTED: Stone Talus, **WANTED: Molduga** , and WANTED: Hinox. 

Accept WANTED: Molduga and Gralens will provide you with the target's map location near the East Barrens within Gerudo Desert. 

## How to Easily Defeat Molduga

If you haven't encountered a Molduga yet, it's a predatory beast that hides in the sand and is attracted to sound. The easiest way to take it out is to find a rocky island or solid area in the sand and use it as a base. Shoot an arrow with a sound-producing attachment fused to it at the nearby sand (within close distance to your protective rock). You can use bombs, of course, but even Brightbloom Seeds do the trick. 

After you've shot the ground, the nearby Molduga will go to the location -- a question mark indicating that the creature has heard the sound. It will then leap out of the sand into the air trying to swallow up its presumed prey. This is your queue: fire a bomb arrow at the exposed belly of the creature and it'll come crashign down to the ground. Run over to its head and strike at it with a powerful weapon repeatedly. Retreat to safety when it wakes up and repeat the procedure. 

Once you have taken out Molduga, collect your spoils, travel back to Lookout Landing and turn in the quest to receive 100 Rupees. 

If this is the third of the WANTED quests you've completed, you'll also receive a Gold Rupee, worth 300.
