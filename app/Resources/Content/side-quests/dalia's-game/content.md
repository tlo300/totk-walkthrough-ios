**Dalia’s Game** is one of the many Side Quests to complete in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know to complete Dalia’s Game, including where to find it and what you will get as a reward. 

  * **Side Quest Location** : Gerudo Town
  * **Prerequisites** : Finishing **The Heroines’ Secret** , starting The Mysterious Eighth, and completing Gerudo’s portion of Regional Phenomena
  * **Rewards** : Orb



If you try to pick up the orb with the golden circle on it hanging out in **Gerudo Town** (during the day; you won’t activate the quest at night), you’ll be stopped by a young Gerudo named **Dalia**. If you’ve started The Mysterious Eighth, the quest can begin since you learn the orb is important. 

In order to win the orb from Dalia, you have to entertain her in a game. She’ll hide one of the stuffed sand-seal dolls somewhere in the **Gerudo Shelter**. You have 90 seconds to find it. 

The plush doll is over by where the spa beds were, in the same area with the map of the town drawn on the wall in chalk. 

Find the doll and Dalia will be satisfied, giving you the orb and allowing you to use it to complete The Mysterious Eighth.
