Gerudo’s Colossal Fossil is one of the many Side Quests to complete in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. In this guide, we will teach you everything you need to know to complete Gerudo’s Colossal Fossil in Zelda TotK, including where to find it and what you will get as a reward. 

  * **Side Quest Location** : Gerudo Great Skeleton, Gerudo Desert
  * **Prerequisites** : Complete **Eldin’s Colossal Fossil** and **Hebra’s Colossal Fossil**
  * **Rewards** : 50 Rupees



## TotK Gerudo's Colossal Fossil Walkthough

Following the events of **Hebra’s Colossal Fossil** , **Loone** will head on over to **Gerudo Great Skeleton** (-4822, -3866, -0003). The path to the cave has some Lizafol ambushes and a Bokoblin tower, so be aware of that. 

Speak to Loone towards the cave entrance once again, where she’ll once again give you the skinny on what needs to be done. This time, you’ll be making a baby skeleton version of the larger creature in this cave (and similar in anatomy to the one found in Hebra). 

The skull is right in front of Loone, but you’ll need to find two other pieces - the ribs/fins and the tail. 

The tailbone is to the right of Loone, stuck in some piles of sand. There’s a Zonai fan nearby if you need it to help clear the sand and get the long bone. 

The torso is stuck at the very stop of the cave. Head towards the very tip of the larger skeleton’s skull and Ascend on top of it, then go along the back of the creature. You should see some ribs sticking out of an extremely rocky ceiling. You’ll need to chuck some bombs (of which there are several Bomb Flowers in this cave as well) to get it loose. 

The ribs and fins should fall to the floor, where you can move them towards the baby skull. 

Unlike the first two missions in this chain, **you’ll have to put the baby skeleton together through Ultrahand attaching**. 

The torso should be attached at the base of the skull with the ribs downward and the fins facing towards the rear, while the tailbone should be attached to the torso’s spine with the spikes facing upwards. 

Once you’ve completed all this and the bones look presentable, speak to Loone again and in her awe thanks you for seeing all this with her and gives you your reward: 50 Rupees.
