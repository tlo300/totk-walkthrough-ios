**Thunderwing Butterflies** are rare creatures in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. They come out in stormy weather to furiously flit about in defiance of the rain. Once caught, they can be used in cooking recipes. Learn where to find Thunderwing Butterflies, tips for catching them, and what cooking effects they provide in TotK with this creature guide. 

![](image-001.jpg)

**Official Description** : "This rare butterfly only shows itself when it rains. The organs in its body produce an insulating compound. When made into an elixir, it offers electrical resistance." 

**Compendium Number:** 75 

075 Thunderwing Butterfly

## Where to Find Thunderwing Butterflies

Thunderwing Butterflies inhabit certain areas that see a lot of rainy weather. They come out in small swarms of three or four, but only when it's raining. 

**Common Locations:**

  * Thunderhead Isles
  * Faron Grasslands



**Specific Sightings:**

  * The best place to find Thunderwing Butterflies is in the Thunderhead Isles above Faron. It's always raining there so the butterflies are always out.



## Where to Buy Thunderwing Butterflies

Thunderwing Butterflies can be purchased for **10 Rupees** at the following shops: 

  * **Beedle** at the Tabantha Bridge Stable.
  * **Beedle** at the Lakeside Stable.
  * **Beedle** at the Foothill Stable.
  * **Beedle** at the Riverside Stable.



## Tips for Catching Thunderwing Butterflies

Thunderwing Butterflies, just like other butterflies, aren't easy to catch. They spook easily and fly quickly. You'll need to be fast if you plan on grabbing one out of the air. 

## Thunderwing Butterfly Effects

  * **Hearts Recovered:** 0
  * **Unique Cooking Effect:** Shock Resistance
  * **Value:** 2
