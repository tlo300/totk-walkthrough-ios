**A Picture for the Closed Stable II** is the second of two picture-taking side quests you can complete at the closed-down Gerudo Canyon Stable. All you need to do is find the right location and snap a picture of the desired scenery and you'll get a reward. 

  * **Side Quest Location:** Gerudo Canyon Stable, Gerudo Desert Gateway
  * **Prerequisites:** Complete Camera Work in the Depths and Piaffe, Packed Away
  * **Rewards:** 1 Pony Point, 1 Spicy Mushroom Risotto



Find Gerudo Canyon Stable at the entrance to the Gerudo Desert. Once you've cleared the room of boxes via the Piaffe, Packed Away quest and learned about the stable's fate and learn that it's no longer in operation, you can look at the two empty frames on the wall. It's a good idea to activate both quests. Piaffe will tell you that he wants a very specific image to fill the frame: 

Picture hint: a photo of a giant sword, wish is said to be found in the Gerudo Highlands. 

## Where is the Forgotten Sword?

Belonging to the Statue of the Eighth Heroine, the giant Forgotten Sword is actually found quite a way off from the stone statue. The Giant Sword is found on Gerudo Summit, in west Hyrule. The exact location is to the south, slightly southeast of the Gerudo Summit plateau. 

## How Do You Get a Picture of the Giant Sword

The easiest way to get to the Giant Sword is to launch yourself into the air from the Gerudo Highlands Skyview Tower. You can easily fast-travel to it once you've unlocked it. Float over to the Gerudo Summit mesa and look for a Gleeok on top of a rock -- a Gleeok is a dangerous, three-headed dragon in case you haven't met one yet. 

Don't worry, though, you don't need to fight it to get the shot. Simply land to the southeast of the Gleeok rock and you can easily see the giant stone sword leaning onto it at an angle. Snap a photo -- a "Forgotten Sword" marker in your viewfinder should confirm that you're taking the right pic. 

Now head back to Gerudo Canyon Stable and walk to the corresponding frame on the wall and active it. Piaffe will tell you about the quest and again and you get to show him the photo. As a reward, you'll get a Pony Point and a Spicy Mushroom Risotto. 

If you haven't done it yet, you can also complete A Picture for Closed Stable I.
