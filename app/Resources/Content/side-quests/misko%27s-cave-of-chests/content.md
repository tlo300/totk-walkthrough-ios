Ready to explore another Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom cave? The Misko's Cave of Chests side quest will send you into the depths of Cephla Lake Cave, accompanied by an adorable little dog. 

Here's how to complete the **Misko's Cave of Chests** side quest. 

## Misko's Cave of Chests Walkthrough

To start this Tears of the Kingdom side quest, go to **Cephla Lake Cave** , west of Cephla Lake and just north of the Foothill Stable in the Eldin region (coordinates 2604, 1322, 0149). Speak to the two men resting in front of the cave, Domidak and Prissen. They'll explain that the cave hides a valuable treasure chest, but it's almost impossible to find it amongst all the dummy treasure chests - which will give you nothing but a single green rupee. 

There are two ways to complete Misko's Cave of Chests: you may go inside and open every single chest until you find the one with the real treasure (using your Ultrahand ability to pull them from the floors and walls), but this will obviously take some time. Alternatively, you can solve this Tears of the Kingdom side quest by **getting help from the nearby dog**. 

The dog is walking around the small camp, just outside of Cephla Lake Cave. Keep dropping meat or fruits for him until he starts walking towards the cave. Follow him inside, and he'll lead you to the real treasure chest, which contains a pair of **Ember Trousers**, part of the Ember Armor Set. 

This completes the Misko's Cave of Chests side quest, but beware: during the final dialogue scene, Domidak and Prissen **leave a glass bottle behind** , which contains a note from none other than the great bandit Misko himself. Interact with this bottle to start the next side quest: Misko's Treasure: The Fierce Deity.
