Time for some Hyrule archeology! The Eldin's Colossal Fossil side quest is the first quest in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom's Colossal Fossil quest series, followed by Hebra's Colossal Fossil and Gerudo's Colossal Fossil. 

Here's how to find the Eldin Great Skeleton and complete **Eldin's Colossal Fossil**. 

## Eldin's Colossal Fossil Walkthrough

To start Eldin's Colossal Fossil, travel to **East Akkala Stable** in the northeastern corner of the map and speak to **Loone** , who's standing near the stable's entrance. 

Next, you need to find a large fossil near the Eldin Mountains. It's directly north of Goron City, at the northernmost border of the map. You can also teleport to the Mayak Shrine if you've already unlocked it, and then go east. The coordinates are 1670, 3650, 0101. 

Speak to Loone, who's waiting near the giant skeleton, and she'll express her disappointment at the fossil's **missing eye**. Luckily, you can find a large boulder underneath the fossil's skull to replace it with. Simply break the loose rocks around it, and use Ultrahand to place the boulder in the empty eye socket. Speak to Loone again to complete Eldin's Colossal Fossil. 

Loone will reward you with one **purple rupee** (worth 50 green rupees) and start the next quest: Hebra's Colossal Fossil.
