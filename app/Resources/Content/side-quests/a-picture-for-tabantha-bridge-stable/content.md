**A Picture for Tabantha Bridge Stable** is one of many Side Quests in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom that requires Link to fetch an image for the stable in question. This side quest begins at Tabantha Bridge Stable in Hyrule Ridge. 

To get the quest started, approach the empty frame with the stable symbol painted in the center. 

  * **Quest Giver** : Dabi
  * **Prerequisite** : None
  * **Reward** : Tap to Reveal



Upon approaching the frame Dabi, the stable owner, explains that The Stable Association members have hung frames in preparation for putting up paintings. 

Dabi asks for a picture featuring the following: 

_"I thought that accenting this place with a picture of the**Ancient Columns** greeting the **sunrise** might please our customers."_

The problem with that, of course, is the Ancient Columns are home to monsters. It's up to you to get a photo for him to use as a reference for the painting. 

### Ancient Columns Location

According to the side quest description, the Ancient Columns are on "the top of the cliff across Tabantha Great Bridge." The bridge is just north of the stable. Getting across is much faster if you grab a horse and follow the road. 

When you cross the bridge from the stable, you'll want to go left and ascend the cliff there. At the top of these cliffs you'll find the Ancient Columns. **Coordinates** : -3464, 0448, 0277. 

Be extremely careful not to go too far west! The major threat near the Ancient Columns is a Gleeok!

If you've arrived with plenty of time till sunrise, don't forget that you can build a campfire to pass the time. To make a campfire, hold one wood bundle and flint then drop them on the ground. Make sure the flint is close to the wood. Then, use a sword to strike the flint into the wood, causing it to catch fire. **Interact with the campfire** and choose to wait till morning! 

Then, take out your camera and take a photo of the columns and the sunrise. The sunrise is around 5 AM. 

Once you have the photo return to Tabantha Bridge Stable. Enter and interact with the empty frame. Dabi will strike up a conversation about it, giving you the chance to show him the photo. 

Select it when prompted and he'll ask if he can have a copy. With that done, he'll reproduce your photo into a painting for the stable. 

Dabi rewards you with One Pony Point and a Hasty Elixir.
